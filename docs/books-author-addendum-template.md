Status: a template, not legal advice and not signed by anyone. The "accompanying notes" checklist mentioned in the build request was not attached, so this follows the topics the system actually needs; please compare it with your own checklist and add anything missing. Bracketed items are blanks for you to fill in. Have it reviewed before use.

**Addendum to the publishing agreement between C-Chu Media Ltd ("the Publisher") and [Author name] ("the Author"), for the book "[Title]" (ISBN [ISBN]).**

1. **Rights confirmation.** The Author confirms that they own or control the rights needed to publish and sell the book in print and as an ebook, and that the book does not infringe anyone else's rights. The Publisher will not list the book for sale until this is confirmed and recorded.
2. **Formats and territories.** The Publisher may sell: [ebook / print / bundle]. Print copies are sold and shipped within Nigeria at launch. Ebooks may be sold [worldwide / in Nigeria only]. [Territory notes.]
3. **Price.** The Publisher sets the selling price in Naira and, where agreed, in US dollars. The Author will be told before a price changes by more than [x]%.
4. **Royalty.** The Author receives: on ebook sales, [__]% of net revenue (the amount received after the payment processor's fee); on print sales, [__]% of the amount received after the payment processor's fee and the printing cost per copy [₦__ per copy, or "as notified"]; on sales that came through the Author's own referral link, [__]% (instead of the standard percentage). The percentages above are the ones entered in the Publisher's system for this book.
5. **Payment.** Royalties become payable [30] days after the sale (to allow for refunds), and are paid [monthly] once the amount payable reaches [₦5,000]. Amounts below the minimum carry over. The Publisher keeps a record of every sale and sends a statement on request. Payment is made by [bank transfer] to the account reference the Author has given.
6. **Refunds.** If a sale is refunded, the Author's royalty on that sale is reversed. If it was already paid, it is deducted from the next payment.
7. **Digital protection.** Ebook files are delivered to buyers with the buyer's name marked on them and a limited number of downloads. The Author agrees not to ask the Publisher to remove this protection.
8. **Sample.** The Publisher may publish a free sample of up to [__ pages / the first chapter] to promote the book.
9. **Author profile.** The Author allows the Publisher to show their name, photograph and biography on the author page and in marketing.
10. **Term and ending.** This addendum lasts [__]. Either side may end the online sale by [30] days' written notice; copies already bought stay available to the buyers.
11. **Data.** The Publisher will not give the Author the personal details of buyers.
12. **Law.** [Governing law and dispute handling.]
