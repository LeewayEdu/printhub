# Proposed changes to the Terms of Service for C-Chu Books: DRAFT

Status: proposals only. The live Terms (`src/app/terms/page.tsx`) have NOT been edited. Wording in brackets needs your decision.

## Add a section "Books and digital products"

1. **What you are buying.** Ebooks are licensed to you for personal use. You may not copy, share, resell or publish the file. Each file you download carries your name and email, and each purchase can be downloaded a limited number of times ([5] at launch).
2. **Delivery.** Ebooks are available in "My books" as soon as your payment is confirmed. You need an account to buy.
3. **Printed books.** Print copies are delivered within Nigeria, or can be collected in Karu. Delivery timing follows the product page. For delivery outside Nigeria, message us on WhatsApp.
4. **Prices and currency.** Prices are shown in Naira. [If USD is switched on: some ebooks are also priced in US dollars for buyers outside Nigeria. The currency you pay in is the one shown at checkout.]
5. **Refunds.** See the digital refund policy (`docs/books-digital-refund-policy-draft.md`): no refund after download except a defective file.
6. **Misuse.** We may withdraw access to a purchased ebook, without refund, if it is shared or resold in breach of these terms.

## Changes to existing sections

- **Orders and delivery:** add that ebook-only orders have no delivery fee and no design-file step.
- **Refunds and reprints:** state that the "free reprint if the error is ours" promise applies to printed books and print products, and that digital products follow the digital refund policy.
- **Privacy:** add that we record download events (time and a one-way hash of the network address, not the address itself) to protect authors' work; and that the buyer's name and email are printed on their copy of an ebook.
- **Intellectual property:** add that authors keep the rights in their books and license C-Chu Media to sell them under a signed author agreement.

## Not decided here
Exact refund window, whether ebook sales outside Nigeria are enabled at launch, and any tax statement for books (no VAT claim is made on book lines in invoices until you confirm the treatment).
